Presented the overview of the 2nd Health Privacy Challenge @ ISMB/CAMDA 2026. 

---

[The 2nd edition Health Privacy Challenge](https://benchmarks.elsa-ai.eu/?ch=8&com=introduction) has been completed! The number of CAMDA extended abstract submissions doubled in this edition, with contributions from both returning and new teams. Participants were invited to present their solutions during the [Health Privacy Session at #CAMDA](https://www.iscb.org/ismb2026/scientific-programme/cosi-tracks-other-abstracts/camda), as part of the ISMB 2026 Conference on July 16, 2025, in Washington, D.C.

Read details at [ELSA Website](https://elsa-ai.eu/recap-elsa-health-privacy-challenge-2026/ )!
